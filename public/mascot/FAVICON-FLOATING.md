## Composition prompt

Subject: one tiny charming brown otter floating on its back, full body visible, head toward the left, body reclining diagonally across the center, two small paws resting on its cream belly, thick tail curling upward toward the right. Small rounded ears, brown fur, cream muzzle and belly, dark friendly eyes. Composition like the familiar floating otter emoji: a compact horizontal curved silhouette, NOT a front-facing head portrait, NOT a bear face. Medium-small animal centered with comfortable lavender space around it, animal occupies about 76 percent of canvas width. Style: simple polished emoji-like illustrated icon, clean softly shaded broad shapes, highly readable at 16 and 32 pixels, minimal details, subtle charming expression. Fully opaque square canvas filled edge-to-edge with solid soft periwinkle lavender (#BBC6F5), no border, no shadow, no text, no letters, no browser chrome, no extra animals or scenery. Do not draw a rounded tile within the canvas: the application will clip the exported favicon to rounded-square corners. One icon only.
